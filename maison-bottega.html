The Case

Six pastries, two countries, no compromises.

We don't fuse anything. The croissant is laminated the way it's laminated in the 6th arrondissement; the sfogliatella is shaped the way it's shaped in Naples. Nothing on this counter apologizes for where it's from.

France

The Croissant

Viennoiserie · Laminated dough

Twenty-seven layers of butter and dough, folded over three days, baked until the outside shatters and the inside pulls apart in ribbons. No filling. It doesn't need one.

Butter82% AOP
Proof18 hours
Best eatenWithin the hour
Italy — Sicily

Cannolo Siciliano

Fried shell · Sweetened ricotta

Fried to order, filled to order — a soggy cannolo is a crime in three regions. Sheep's-milk ricotta, drained overnight, folded with candied orange and a little too much sugar.

ShellMarsala wine dough
FillingSheep ricotta
OriginPalermo, 12th c.
France

Mille-Feuille

Puff pastry · Crème pâtissière

"A thousand leaves," though we've never counted. Three sheets of puff, two of vanilla cream, a fondant top scored into the classic feathered pattern. Eaten with a fork, argued about with a spoon.

Layers729, in theory
CreamBourbon vanilla
Also calledNapoleon
Italy — Campania

Sfogliatella

Shell-shaped · Semolina & ricotta

Hand-shaped into its clam ridges, filled with semolina, ricotta and candied citrus, baked until the edges char slightly. Named "riccia" for its curls. Eats loudly. That's correct.

ShapeRiccia (curly)
FillingSemolina-ricotta
OriginNaples, 1600s
Italy — Veneto

Tiramisù

Ladyfingers · Espresso · Mascarpone

Savoiardi dipped fast — one second too long and the whole tray turns to soup — layered with mascarpone and a dusting of cocoa that should never, ever be sweetened.

Name means"Pick me up"
CoffeeDouble espresso
Rest12 hours, minimum
France

Paris-Brest

Choux ring · Praline cream

Baked as a wheel in honor of the bicycle race between Paris and Brest, sliced open and packed with hazelnut praline cream. Heavier than it looks. Intentionally.

Invented1910, Maisons-Laffitte
CreamPraline mousseline
ShapeBicycle wheel
